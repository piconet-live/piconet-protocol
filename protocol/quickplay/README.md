# protocol/quickplay

Quick Play's rotation (piconet-docs `research/game-modes/`, design 6): `quickplay.toml`, the coordinator's list of
mode files, and the official mode files it names, and a custom lobby's official modes. A mode file is one choice: its settings are its parameters on their defaults, which a custom lobby's host changes (Quick Play's normal mode: its laps, CPU level, boost, mines, finish limit, machine setting and stats); a variant is a file of its own. Every rule of the race is the mode's: a word left out is the game's own way. A vote card is a (course, mode) pair: five course cards, two
of them spicy (two different spicy options, each on its own course), and Random, which lands on a spicy option at `random_spicy_chance`. The cards and Random take from a lobby's shuffle bags (`ShuffleBag`, `RotationBags`): one of the spicy options, each at its weight, and one of courses for every mode, so every course is offered once in a mode before any is offered in it again, and the spicy options take turns. `Online.Protocol`
`QuickPlayRotation` reads the folder and compiles each mode with the one compiler (`ModeCompiler`); the service reads
it at its start (a file that does not load stops the start), and the launcher's stand-in service races the same.

Every word a mode file can use: [MODE-REFERENCE.md](../../MODE-REFERENCE.md).

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `quickplay.toml` | The rotation: the card counts, the normal option and the spicy options with their weights. The courses are each mode file's `courses` word (left out: the pool). Also `lobby_modes`: a custom lobby's official modes in the order a host is offered them (Quick Play's normal mode, then the others); the server lists those it can race (`LobbyList.Modes`) |
| `competitive.fzmode` | The normal option: the game's own rules with the competitive scene's laps (`laps by course`), on every course of the pool; three lives each (`lives`: every wreck the game restores costs one, as in Death Race; the last is final); its parameters (Laps "By course" or a number, CPU level, boost, mines, finish limit, machine setting, stats, CPU racers Off, Vote or Always, Lives 1 to 9) are what a custom lobby's host may change, on Quick Play's values by default |
| `knockout.fzmode` | A spicy option: a rolling start, then the last racer is out every interval, on any course of the pool; F-Zero AX's HUD (`hud_layout ax`: the position bottom left, the cut clock at AX's timer place) and the position and faces follow the mode's places during the race (`live_places`: the racers still in by the race order, then the racers out); a host may set CPU racers Off, Vote or Always (Vote unless changed) and the CPU level (Master unless changed) |
| `practice.fzmode` | Not in the rotation: Play's Practice, raced alone (`solo`); the launcher starts it itself, beside the program in this folder |
| `time_trial.fzmode` | Not in the rotation: a time trial, raced alone (`solo`); no launcher page starts it yet |
| `ghost_race.fzmode` | A spicy option: a time trial raced together: everyone starts on one spot, nobody touches anybody, the others drawn as ghosts; on any course of the pool, with Quick Play's laps by course (its own copy of `competitive.fzmode`'s table, kept alike by `QuickPlayRotationTests`; Laps "By course" or a number); a custom lobby's host may choose it |
| `overdrive.fzmode` | A spicy option: a rolling start at 1000 km/h, then a target speed that rises 120 km/h every 10 s (Speed added every 10 s, 5 to 150), a little more every step, at most 9000; the driving is Twilight's survival mod (the library's `drive`): the engine's speed is pulled towards the target, the brake lowers it and costs energy (Brake energy cost, 0 to 200%, 100 unless changed), a boost surges above it (Boost surge, 0 to 50%, 20 unless changed), the top speed stat open (`max_speed_open`, `seat_speed_open`); a racer that stalls (less than a share of the target's distance in a window) overloads and explodes; racers are out the normal way and the race ends with the last one in; scored by distance: the whole kilometres made from GO as points (`results points`, headed DISTANCE, `results_times off`), the places following the metres during the race (`live_places`); DISTANCE reads the metres driven since GO (`show_distance`, `distance_label off`); on any course of the pool; a custom lobby's host may choose it; a host may set CPU racers Off, Vote or Always (Vote unless changed) and the CPU level (Master unless changed) |
| `death_race.fzmode` | A spicy option, on eleven courses (its `courses` word, live's list: Twist Road, Split Oval, Surface Slide, Loop Cross, Drift Highway, Mobius Ring, Undulation, Screw Drive, Meteor Stream, Sonic Oval, Emperor's Trial): F-Zero X's Death Race, no finish line, three lives each (the game restores a wreck; the last is final), boosts at half their energy (seat.stat.boost_energy), on a six-minute clock; points for every KO the game credits, for staying in and for each life left, a bonus for the first three still in; damage 400% with `damage_cap off`; F-Zero AX's HUD (`hud_layout ax`, the lives counter placed at the bottom centre with `hud_move`, the points so far in place of the race time and lap times with `show_score`) and the position and faces follow the points order during the race (`live_places`); a custom lobby's host may choose it; a host may set CPU racers Off, Vote or Always (Vote unless changed) and the CPU level (Master unless changed) |
| `crown.fzmode` | A spicy option: Crown (the owner's, 8 November): one racer holds the crown and scores a point every second they keep it; touch the holder to take it; on a four-minute clock (Race length, 2 to 8 min); the first crown goes to a racer drawn at random 8 s after GO (First crown); the taker is untouchable and faster for a while (Safe after taking it 4 s, Speed after taking it 25%), then runs 60% slower under their own stats (Holder slower by); a holder who hides, parks or drives backwards (less than 400 km/h forwards each 3 s, Holder must keep moving; backwards a debt) or wrecks loses it to last place; restore on, nobody is out; the points so far where the race time was (`show_score`, POINTS) and no lap times; the course map shows each racer only themselves and the holder; F-Zero AX's HUD (`hud_layout ax`) and the position and faces follow the points order during the race (`live_places`: most points, the holder first among equals, then the race order); on any course of the pool; most points at the end wins; a custom lobby's host may choose it |
